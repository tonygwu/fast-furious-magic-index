The Fast & Furious
Magic Index

One Magic Unit (MU) is one order of magnitude of improbability. MU = −log10(P), where P is the chance a stunt ends the way the film shows it, if the best real-world professional attempts it with the equipment on screen. 1 in 10 is 1 MU. 1 in a million is 6.

Every number here is built from named components with stated evidence, and the whole dataset, the scoring engine and these worksheets are public on GitHub: github.com/tonygwu/fast-furious-magic-index. Disagree with an assumption and you can change one line and rebuild.
