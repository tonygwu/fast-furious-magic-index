### ff01-c01 Opening truck hijacking — boarding a moving semi at highway speed
- sequence: Opening truck hijacking (Blu-ray chapter "The Hijackers")
- approx_time: 0:00–0:07 (estimate; hard anchor: scene is confirmed at 00:00:00 start of the theatrical cut, movie-censorship.com)
- characters: Dominic Toretto (driver, inferred — lead Civic), Vince (performer — boards the truck), Leon (driver, inferred), Letty Ortiz (driver, inferred), unnamed trucker (survivor)
- depicted: Three heavily modified black Honda Civics chase a semi-truck trailer carrying electronic goods on the Terminal Island Freeway near the Port of Los Angeles at night. One crew member (Vince) fires a grappling hook that shatters the truck's windshield, fires a second line into the cab, and uses the cable to pull himself from his own moving Civic into the moving truck's cab while both vehicles are at highway speed. The driver fights back with a baseball bat; he is subdued (tranquilized, per one source) and the crew takes control of the truck. The hijacking succeeds.
- inferred: The individual identities of the other two Civic drivers (Dom, Leon, Letty) are not stated on-screen at this point in the story — the audience only learns later that this crew is Dom's; the attribution here follows the Fast & Furious Wiki and later-film dialogue. The exact method of transferring cargo from the truck to the trailing Civics (moving handoff vs. stopping first) is not confirmed by any source found — flagged as an open question.
- real_world_params: cargo reported as electronic goods/DVD players "from China," one fan-wiki figure of "$1.6 million" (fastandfurious.fandom.com/wiki/Assault_on_the_Rodgers_Truck, wiki-claim, unverified against a second source — treat cautiously); location = Terminal Island Freeway/LA port area (film); stunt vehicle "Mic Rig," built by stunt coordinator Mic Rodgers with SFX coordinator Matt Sweeney specifically for vehicle-to-vehicle stunt shots, later won Rodgers a 2002 Academy Award for Technical Achievement (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mic_Rodgers, BTS/analysis).
- sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fast_and_the_Furious_(2001_film), https://fastandfurious.fandom.com/wiki/Assault_on_the_Rodgers_Truck (snippet only, page unread)
- sources_ok: true
- post_credit: false
- notes / disagreements: An early draft screenplay (scriptslug PDF, dated May 2000, four months before principal photography began) stages this scene completely differently — a masked "Bandit" firing a speargun, riding the hood like "an insane surfer," and using a chemical smoke grenade — with four Civics, not three. I could not confirm whether this draft was ever shot; it may be an earlier, unused concept. Treat the grappling-hook/tranquilizer version (Wikipedia + Fandom, matching all recap sources and matching the actually-released trailer/clip titles found in search results) as what the theatrical cut shows, and flag the screenplay draft only as background color on how elaborate the writers' original pitch was.

## Rejected (considered, ordinary)
- Brian's Eclipse suffers a NOS/engine failure during the first race — an ordinary mechanical failure (over-boosted nitrous systems do fail in real drag racing); no one is harmed and no improbable feat or survival is being tested.
- Johnny Tran and Lance destroy Brian's parked/idling Eclipse with baseball bats — straightforward vandalism/intimidation, no physical stunt or survival element.
- The Jesse-vs-Tran drag race itself (Jetta vs. S2000) — a clean, uneventful race with a clear loser; no crash or survival event is depicted in the race itself (Jesse's death is a separate, later event, covered as ff01-c06).
- Police/SWAT action against Johnny Tran's garage and his brief arrest — ordinary law-enforcement procedure.
- Family dinner/backyard barbecue scenes — no physical stunt content.
- Brian's undercover investigative work (garage snooping, surveillance) — ordinary investigative activity, no physical improbability.
- Producer Neal H. Moritz's cameo losing a race in a Ferrari F355 at Race Wars — a brief comedic aside; no crash or improbable survival found in any source.
