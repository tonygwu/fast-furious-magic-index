  ~80 mi NE of Los Angeles (real location, not the in-story Arizona setting, https://movie-locations.com/movies/f/Fast-And-The-Furious-Tokyo-Drift.php);

## Rejected (considered, ordinary)

- DK (Takashi) physically assaults/beats Sean after learning about Neela — an ordinary one-sided beating with no depicted physical impossibility; not a candidate for elite-attempt MU scoring.
- Comedic debt-collection errands Sean runs for Han (e.g., collecting money from an intimidatingly large man) — no vehicle/physical stunt involved.
- "Drifting date": Neela drives Sean through a forest/mountain road at night — a demonstration drift with no reported crash or near-miss in any source reached; ordinary within the film's own baseline of skilled driving.
- Rapid mastery of drifting: Sean goes from total novice to competition-beating drifter across what the film implies is a matter of weeks. This is a real implausibility but is a training-montage/time-compression trope, not a single physical event with a computable "elite professional attempts it" probability — flagged instead in Open questions.
- Full restoration/build of the 1967 Mustang for the final race, completed in an implausibly short time by Han's old crew — same time-compression issue as above, not a discrete physical stunt.
- Yubitsume (finger-cutting) reference — production added missing fingers to a Yakuza character digitally for cultural accuracy; this is a static character detail, not a depicted stunt or event.
- Sean's initial confrontation with Takashi at the car meet (verbal/social conflict escalating to the race challenge) — no physical event yet at that point.
