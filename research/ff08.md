Credits start: **not confirmed — two independent estimation methods disagree, flagged per the brief's
rule on disagreeing sources.** Method 1 (subtitle-file timing probe): last spoken line times out at
approx. 134.5 min, implying only ~1.5 min of end-crawl, which is short for a tentpole action film —
possibly the ripped subtitle file is a shade shorter than the true theatrical print, or the 136-min
figure is rounded. Method 2 (genre-typical heuristic): no F8-specific source found, so this method
estimates 127–129 min by analogy to prior franchise entries' typical 7–9 minute end-crawl — explicitly
NOT anchored to this film. Treat the true value as unknown, somewhere in the 127–134.5 min range.

**No mid- or post-credits scene exists in the theatrical cut.** This is well corroborated: Universal
filmed a Hobbs/Shaw stinger (no Dom) but Vin Diesel had it pulled before release, per
https://www.thewrap.com/fate-furious-f8-post-credits-scene/ ("'Fate of the Furious' has no post-credits

### ff08-c01 Havana "Cuban mile" street race
- sequence: Havana honeymoon race (pre-title)
- approx_time: ~10–16 min (presence-log estimate; cold open before main titles)
- characters: Dom Toretto (driver, of Fernando's car — see disagreement note), Raldo (driver, of his own
  car; loan shark/racer), Fernando (beneficiary — Dom's cousin, whose debt is the stake), Letty Ortiz
  (survivor/beneficiary — watches)
- depicted: Dom challenges Raldo to a straight-line street race to erase Fernando's debt, driving
  Fernando's stripped-down 1949 Chevrolet Fleetline (seats, doors, and battery removed; nitrous fed by
  pulling a vacuum line — described in-universe as "Cuban NOS") against Raldo's 1956 Ford Fairlane Crown
  Victoria, with Dom's own 1961 Chevrolet Impala wagered as a side stake. Film dialogue explicitly frames
  the distance as non-standard: "We ain't racing a quarter mile here. We race the Cuban mile." Mid-race, a
  hired motorcycle rider deliberately collides with Dom's car (see ff08-c02). The engine overheats and
  catches fire; Dom keeps control, and reverses the burning car across the finish line to win. He gives
  Fernando the Impala afterward.
- inferred: "The Cuban mile" is not a real standardized distance unit; a UC San Diego politics
  professor's published commentary on the film (cited in Wikipedia's Reception section) glosses the race
  as "one-mile," which is an outside interpretation, not a film-stated figure.
- real_world_params: stock 1949 Chevrolet Fleetline curb weight = 3,155 lb / 1,431 kg (location, real
  vehicle pre-modification baseline, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chevrolet_Fleetline); stock Fleetline
  engine = 216 cu in (3.5 L) 90 hp inline-6, later 235.5 cu in / 105 hp option (real vehicle, same
  source); stock Fleetline top speed "could easily exceed 80 mph without overdrive," no 0–60 figure given
  (real vehicle, same source); the film car is heavily modified (weight-stripped + nitrous), so none of
  these stock numbers describe its as-raced performance — no dyno/lap-time figure for the modified movie
  car was found; 1956 Ford Fairlane Crown Victoria (Raldo's car) real specs = unknown (searched, did not
  locate the correct Wikipedia page before the search budget ran out).
- sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fate_of_the_Furious ; https://www.springfieldspringfield.co.uk/movie_script.php?movie=the-fate-of-the-furious (dialogue quote) ; https://www.fandango.com/movie-news/fast-furious-and-delirious-in-havana-752442 (car-build detail, reached via search snippet only) ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chevrolet_Fleetline
- sources_ok: true
- post_credit: false
- notes / disagreements: whether Dom personally drives Fernando's car (vs. Fernando driving it) is not
  fully settled — the script-transcript tool gave inconsistent answers across repeated queries, and
  Wikipedia's plot summary is unambiguous only that the race is Fernando's Fleetline vs. Raldo's Fairlane
  with the Impala as a side wager. Flagged as an open point.
