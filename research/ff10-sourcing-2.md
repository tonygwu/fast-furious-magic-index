# Fast X (2023), second sourcing pass, 2026-09-25

> **Provenance.** A second research pass on Fast X, which the first pass left under-sourced. It records what each
> source says, which candidates now meet the two-source rule, and what could not be confirmed. The scored results are
> in data/events/ff10.yaml and data/rejected.yaml.

## Method and source rules

- Every quote below was checked against the full text of the page it comes from. Search-result excerpts and
  generated summaries were used only to find pages, never as sources.
- **Independence caveats. Adopted as project policy (see docs/DECISIONS.md):**
  - `ff10-fandom` is the Fast X page on fastandfurious.fandom.com. Other pages on the same wiki, such as "Rio Street Race",
    "Chase in Portugal", "Jakob Toretto" and "Isabel Neves", come from the same editor community. They are listed, but they are
    **not** counted as independent of ff10-fandom. None of the "meets 2 sources" verdicts below depends on them.
  - hero.fandom.com's Jakob page copies the Fast & Furious wiki's Jakob sentence almost word for word ("small bottles of
    alcohol ... power a jet engine ... cargo hold"). It is **not** independent. Its Isabel page is worded differently.
  - The ScreenRant helicopter-clip article is from the same outlet as `ff10-screenrant-ridiculous`, although it has a
    different author and a different subject. It is treated as a weak second source and never as the only second source.
- **Unavailable:** several plot-summary, parental-guide and transcript sites could not be read. **No transcript or
  subtitle source was used.**
- **ScreenRant ridiculous list:** the served HTML has only 11 of its 15 entries. The top four did not load, and page `/2/`
  serves the same 11.

## 1. rj-ff10-09 "Two helicopters made to collide". **Now meets 2+ independent sources.**

**What happens.** Dante has already pulled Little B into his own car in the tunnel, so Dom is alone in the 1970 Charger.
Two helicopters rise on either side of an elevated two-deck highway and fire harpoons on cables into the Charger to lift it
off the road. Dom hits the NOS and jumps from the upper deck to the lower deck, and the two helicopters, still tethered to
the car, collide and crash. Dom then spins the car and swings the burning, tethered wrecks like flails into Dante's cars.

- **befores & afters (DNEG VFX sup Francois Lambert):** "In this part of the sequence, two helicopters ascend on each side
  of a highway bridge, aiming to shoot harpoons at Dom's Charger and throw him off the bridge. However, Dom retaliates by
  activating his NOS, causing the helicopters to collide in mid-air and crash." Also: "Dom utilizes the wreckage of the
  crashed helicopters, still attached by wires, as powerful bludgeoning weapons against the mercenary cars."
- **Looper:** "During this sequence, Dante throws everything at Dom — including helicopters that harpoon Dom's car to slow
  him down. ... he's able to drive his car so fast that the harpooned helicopters crash into each other and explode. Dom is
  then able to do donuts in his car using the tethered helicopters like two giant Medieval flails that slam into Dante's
  muscle car with Little Brian inside."
- **SYFY Wire:** "a pair of helicopters brandishing harpoon guns meant to spear his Charger and lift it off the ground via
  high-tension cables." / "Toretto floods the engine with NOS, uses the burst of speed to crash the helicopters into each
  other, and turns the flaming wreckage to his advantage."
- **themoviespoiler (ff10-themoviespoiler, already cited):** "Dom is dropped out of a jet in his car before two of Dante's
  sniper's choppers shoot his car with harpoons! But then Dom drives off the bridge and makes them collide and EXPLODE!"
- **Collider, quoting 2nd-unit stunt coordinator Jack Gill:** "We're on a locked down highway, big highway with two lanes on
  each side." ... "these two helicopters coming in on either side and they skewer into the side of Dom's car and try pick
  him up. But Dom keeps fighting them like crazy and as he gets back to hit one last time, he hits and jumps from one
  freeway over to the other freeway. Lands on the other side, helicopters land in a mid-burning haul behind him."
- The Fast & Furious wiki "Chase in Portugal" page is the same wiki as ff10-fandom: "Dante orchestrated two helicopters to
  lift Dom's car off the ground ... Dom used the nitrous tanks in his car to drive off to the highway below, destroying the
  helicopters. Dom then swung his car around and used the helicopters to damage Dante's cars."

**Who is in the car.** Dom alone. Little B is in Dante's car: Looper says "slam into Dante's muscle car with Little Brian
inside", and Chase in Portugal says Brian "was kidnapped by Dante who pulled him into his car" in the tunnel before the
helicopters.

**Disagreements.**
- Sources differ on whether the helicopters collide *because of* the jump or because of NOS speed. B&A and SYFY say NOS.
  themoviespoiler and the Fandom page say he drives off the bridge or down to the highway below. Gill describes both a hit
  and the jump. These accounts are compatible: the NOS burst carries the car off the upper deck.
- Looper's "slam into Dante's muscle car" and B&A's "against the mercenary cars" name different targets for the flails.

**Real-world numbers and production facts.**
- The helicopters, the collision and the cable connection were **not practical**. ScreenRant clip piece: "While two
  helicopters were not actually smashed together or ever actually connected to the vehicle, the video shows that two
  helicopters were actually flown behind Dom's Charger, which was hooked up to a tow truck to simulate it being connected to
  cables. The Charger's jump from one portion of the bridge to the other was also accomplished in part using practical
  methods, with the car launched via air cannon".
- B&A: "a practical empty Charger was projected from the top deck of the highway onto the lower deck. The car crashed beyond
  repair". Two real helicopters were "piloted by Fred North and his skilled team ... around the city of Vila Real", and
  "both of them were grounded for the climatic moment in which both helicopters crashed together."
- **Helicopter type: not found.** No source names the model. **Cable length, deck height, jump distance and speed: not
  found.** An unsourced claim that the car was puppeted on "a flatbed crane moving at about 40 miles per hour" appears
  on no page that was read, so it is not used.
- Location: A24 motorway, Vila Real district. ScreenRant's locations piece says Vouzela; sceen-it.com says "between
  Vilarinho da Samardã (Vila Real) and Vila Pouca de Aguiar". This is a minor disagreement about which stretch of the A24
  was used.

## 2. rj-ff10-07 "Dom flips Isabel's car to dislodge the bomb". **The flip now meets 2 independent sources. The exact mechanism is thin.**

- **LADbible, Daniela Melchior (the actress playing Isabel):** "My last day of shooting I was doing that scene where my car
  flips during the race, and we really had my full car but with no wheels and with no glass, so I really did the 360-degree
  turns." / "And then we left the car upside down just for a couple of minutes, and they would just shake the car and I had
  to pretend that I was doing the [...] slide."
- **themoviespoiler:** "Dante then tries to go for Isabel anyway, but Dom knocks the bomb off her car despite making her
  crash. Dom pulls her out of the wreck while Dante gets away." It also says earlier: "Dom ends up trying to slow Isabel down,
  making Dante blow up Diogo".
- Same wiki as ff10-fandom, Rio Street Race: "Dom was able to flip her car over, knocking the explosive off and pulled her
  out before Dante could kill her."
- Same wiki, Isabel Neves page: "Dom ran his car into hers causing the explosive to detach and he saved Isabel's life."
- hero.fandom.com, Isabel Neves (a separate wiki with different wording): "Dom manages to dislodge the bomb from Isabel's car
  before it explodes. However, the explosion sets Isabel's car on fire trapping her inside and Dom is forced to throw the race
  to save her."

**Verdict.** Two independent sources support the flip: LADbible, from the actress, and themoviespoiler, which has the crash
and the bomb knocked off. The Fandom page is a third, from the same wiki as ff10-fandom. The mechanism appears to be that
Dom rams or nudges her car, it rolls, and the bomb comes free. Only Fandom pages state "ran his car into hers" or "flip her
car over" as a direct cause.

**Does the bomb fall free?** themoviespoiler, Fandom and the Heroes wiki all say yes. The Heroes wiki adds that it then
explodes and sets her car on fire. That fire detail is single-sourced.

**Does Isabel survive unhurt?** She survives. Every source says Dom pulls her out. None says she is unhurt. themoviespoiler
says next: "Isabel wakes up in Dom's apartment", which suggests she was out or resting. The Heroes wiki says she was trapped
in a burning car. No source gives an injury.

**Numbers.** None. There is no race speed and no bomb yield. Car per Fandom: Isabel's 1975 Datsun 240Z, status "Destroyed".
Melchior's quote shows that the roll was a practical full car body on a rotisserie rig, and this was not a real rollover at
speed.

## 3. rj-ff10-03 "Dante hacks the convoy trucks". **Two independent sources now describe remote control of the bomb truck. The "veer into traffic" detail is still single-sourced. This is probably not a separate physical stunt.**

- **Territory Studio (the film's screen-graphics vendor):** "He uses remote access technology to control a truck containing
  the bomb, and can arm and detonate it once it's in place." / "Dante's technology provides him with surveillance footage of
  the truck from several live camera feeds, as well as maps and other key data points for driving."
- **themoviespoiler:** "Dante then hacks into their trucks to make them veer into traffic before attempting to deliver the
  bomb to The Vatican."
- **villains.fandom.com Dante_Reyes:** "Dante locks Tej and Ramsey in the truck in which he planted a secret bomb ...
  Dante then triggers multiple explosions throughout Rome and then release the bomb".
- Same wiki as ff10-fandom, Attack in Rome: "Tej and Ramsey jumped onto the truck ... incapacitated the drivers using tear
  gas. Ramsey and Tej got into the truck and were then locked inside by Dante who began piloting the truck remotley. As they
  reached crowded streets, Dante set off multiple explosions".
- **ScreenRant ridiculous list:** "he elects to remotely open the doors of the truck and let the bomb roll".

**Verdict.** The beat is real: Dante remotely drives the bomb truck with Tej and Ramsey locked inside. No source describes a
distinct stunt such as a crash, a flip or a collision count. "Veer into traffic" comes from themoviespoiler only. This looks
like the lead-in to the scored rolling-bomb event, a remote-control technology beat rather than a physics feat. The recommendation is
merging it into the rolling-bomb event or keeping it rejected as ordinary, instead of scoring it separately.

**Numbers.** None. Fandom lists the truck as a 2014 Renault T-Series, driver Ramsey, "Destroyed".

## 4. rj-ff10-05 "Jakob slams a man through a floor". **Now meets 3 independent sources.**

- **themoviespoiler (already cited; the existing notes missed it):** "He throws a guy through the floor and bashes other
  villains all around."
- **Looper:** "This entire sequence culminates with an amazing shot of Jakob throwing a corrupt Agency goon through the floor
  of the second story as the camera follows the downed agent almost all the way to the floor."
- **ScreenRant ridiculous (already cited):** "he power slams one opponent through the floor of the second story of the
  house. While the floor might have been loosened up a bit from the spray of bullets sent through the floor..."
- Same wiki as ff10-fandom, Jakob Toretto: "He dropped an [[Agency]] member opponent through the floor of the second story of
  the [[1327|Toretto house]]."

**Details.** Place: the Toretto house in LA, from the second floor down to the ground floor. Victim: an unnamed Agency
operative. His outcome is not stated anywhere. ScreenRant suggests that gunfire had already weakened the floor.

**Disagreement.** The verb varies: "throws" (themoviespoiler, Looper), "power slams" (ScreenRant), "dropped" (Fandom).
ScreenRant says "Dante's henchmen", while Looper and Fandom say Agency operatives. Wikipedia also says "Agency agents".

**Numbers.** None. The fall is one storey, roughly 3 m, but that is an estimate and no source gives it.

## 5. rj-ff10-06 airliner escape. **The escape meets many independent sources. The aircraft type is disputed: glider versus jet.**

**Confirmed across sources.** The flight attendant is Meadow Walker, uncredited. She gives Jakob a key and small bottles of
alcohol. Jakob fights off goons at the back of the plane. He and Little B go to the cargo hold, and a small aircraft
smuggled aboard carries them out mid-flight.

- **Looper:** "While on the plane, Jakob nonchalantly asks a stewardess for some alcohol, which he receives alongside a
  mysterious key. He then beckons Little Brian to the back of the plane with him, where he quickly dispatches some Agency
  goons in plainclothes." / "Afterward, in the cargo hold, Jakob unzips his kayak, revealing a miniature Batman-style spy
  glider which he quickly assembles. Then, he uses the alcohol as quick fuel to help with their descent. Finally, he uses the
  mysterious key to open up the cargo hatch, and he and Brian fly out of the 747 in the glider."
- **Dexerto:** "Walker's flight attendant walks over and hands him a key and three miniature bottles of vodka. 'It's good to
  have friends in high places,' Jakob says." / "As they make their way to the back of the plane, Jakob batters a few goons who
  try to stop them. By the time Walker's character walks down the aisle, they're already gone, leaving the key in place for
  her to pick up."
- **The Direct:** "Meadow's unnamed character gave Jakob a key and three bottles of alcohol, leading to the pair's thrilling
  escape." / "before Jakob and Little Brian managed to get on the jet, John Cena's Fast 10 character had to defeat three agents
  from The Agency."
- **CBR:** "she is one of Jakob's contacts who helps him smuggle a small glider into the plane's cargo hold."
- **Hollywood Reporter review (Frank Scheck):** "the pair escaping a passenger plane filled with bad guys by taking off in,
  what else, a smaller plane located in the cargo hold."
- **Nerdist:** "Did they have a tiny little glider like Jakob?"
- **ScreenRant ridiculous (already cited):** "covertly depart a commercial airliner in a small aircraft hidden in the cargo
  bay ... how it is perfectly suited for both he and Brian to breath in at 36,000 feet is never explained".
- **themoviespoiler:** "a flight attendant (Meadow Walker) who is Jakob's friend alerts him to the presence of three of
  Dante's goons. He knocks them out and has the woman hide their bodies while he and Little Brian board a secret jet toward
  Brazil."
- Same wiki as ff10-fandom, Jakob Toretto (the Brian Marcos page is identical): "Whilst on the plane, Jakob was given small
  bottles of liquor from a flight attendant associate of his and used it to power a jet that he had stored in the Cargo Hold
  of the plane. After fighting off enemies who were sent by the Agency, Jakob and Little B escaped the plane in the jet."

**Answers to the specific questions.**
- **Aircraft type:** disputed. "Glider" comes from Looper ("miniature Batman-style spy glider" assembled from a kayak bag),
  CBR and Nerdist. "Jet" comes from themoviespoiler, The Direct and Fandom. "Smaller plane" or "small aircraft" comes from THR
  and ScreenRant. A powered glider or motor-glider would fit both "glider" and "fuel", but no source says that. **Do not pick
  one without viewing.** The kayak detail has one source, Looper. Looper earlier says that Jakob's '90s car has "a kayak on
  top", which supports it indirectly.
- **Liquor as fuel: confirmed as depicted.** Two independent sources say it. Looper: "uses the alcohol as quick fuel". Fandom:
  "used it to power a jet". Dexerto and The Direct confirm the bottles, "three miniature bottles of vodka", without the fuel
  use. It is not a paraphrasing error.
- **Launch:** Looper says "uses the mysterious key to open up the cargo hatch, and he and Brian fly out". That is one source
  for the key opening the hatch. Every source says the escape happens mid-flight, and The Direct says "escape mid-flight".
- **Airliner type and altitude:** Looper says "747", one source. ScreenRant says "36,000 feet", one source, and it is the
  reviewer's figure, not a number stated in the film. Nothing else gives the phase of flight.
- **Landing place:** no source describes a landing. Destination: "toward Brazil" (themoviespoiler) against Portugal
  (Wikipedia: "Jakob takes Little B to a safe location in Portugal"; Fandom Chase in Portugal: "Jakob and Brian had arrived in
  Portugal after escaping from the commercial flight"). themoviespoiler is probably wrong here.
- **Who is aboard the small aircraft:** Jakob and Little B only. Every source agrees.
- **Goons:** "three" in The Direct and themoviespoiler. themoviespoiler says Dante's goons, while The Direct, Looper and
  Fandom say Agency.

Other facts that may help scoring:
- **Little B's age.** Fandom Brian Marcos page: "Brian is confirmed to be eight years old in Fast X". The research notes say

- **Dam wording.** Looper: "if we use the Hoover Dam for a reference, could be more than 700 feet high". That is a
  hypothetical comparison, not a claim that the dam is Hoover. Keep The Ringer's 460 ft Aldeadávila figure.
- **Portugal drop.** B&A: "In the story, the plane is unable to find a landing spot, leading Dom to drop his Charger onto the

- **Rome shockwave reference.** B&A, Pejic and Lambert: "we looked at real life references of tornadoes and hurricanes that
  reach up to 150 mph". That is the VFX team's reference, not a figure stated in the film.

- The small aircraft's type (glider or jet), where it lands, the altitude beyond ScreenRant's single "36,000 feet", and the
  airliner type beyond Looper's single "747".
- Any injury to Isabel. Any number for the Rio race.
- A second source for N4 (Charger shoved off the upper deck), N7, N8 and N11.
- No transcript or subtitle source could be read.
