### ff04-c10 First tunnel run ends in collapse/explosion; team outruns it
- sequence: First border-tunnel drug run (climax)
- approx_time: ~62–63 min (ESTIMATE)
- characters: Dominic Toretto (driver, survivor), Brian O'Conner (driver, survivor)

- inferred: none beyond the aggregation above.
- real_world_params: Real US–Mexico border smuggling tunnels found by DEA/Border Patrol are typically pedestrian-scale, not vehicle-scale: examples include a 2006 Tijuana–San Diego tunnel 2,400 ft (730 m) long and 5 ft (1.5 m) high at up to 90 ft (27 m) deep, a 2010 Tijuana–Otay Mesa tunnel 2,600 ft (790 m) long, a 2014 Nogales tunnel 481 ft (147 m) long, and a 2012 Nogales tunnel just 3 ft in diameter and 100 yards long (source: Wikipedia Drug_tunnel, real-world).
